How We Make a Difference

At Ngiro Treasures, we believe business should be a force for good. We are deeply committed to the wellbeing of the Samburu people and the pristine ecosystems of Mount Ngiro. Our operations are designed around a simple principle: every jar of honey sold should leave both people and planet better off.

Fair Trade Sourcing

We work directly with local beekeepers, paying fair prices that far exceed the market average. This ensures that families can afford education, healthcare, and a dignified livelihood.

  • Supporting local beekeepers with fair trade pricing
  • Empowering traditional herbal collectors

Environmental Conservation

Our sustainable wild harvesting methods respect the delicate balance of the mountain's ecosystem. We take only what the forest can give, ensuring the bees thrive and biodiversity is preserved.

  • Practicing 100% sustainable wild harvesting
  • Protecting Mount Ngiro's biodiversity

Preserving Traditional Knowledge

The Samburu people possess centuries of knowledge about medicinal herbs and natural healing. By bringing these products to the global market, we help ensure this wisdom is valued, documented, and passed on to future generations.

  • Preserving Samburu traditional knowledge
  • Documenting indigenous herbal remedies
