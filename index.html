Our Heritage

Sacred Wellness from the Slopes of Mount Ngiro

Rising from the heart of Samburu County, Mount Ngiro has long been revered by local communities as a sacred mountain rich in healing plants and natural abundance. For generations, Samburu elders have relied on the mountain's herbs and wild honey for nourishment, medicine, and cultural rituals.

Ngiro Treasures was founded to preserve this heritage and share the mountain's natural gifts with the world.

The Sacred Mountain

Mount Ngiro is one of the most ecologically unique mountains in northern Kenya. Its forests host rare medicinal plants, bees, and wildlife that thrive in this isolated ecosystem.

The Samburu people have protected this mountain for generations, preserving traditional knowledge of herbal medicine and sustainable harvesting.
