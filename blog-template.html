The Miraculous Health Benefits of Raw Wild Honey

Published on August 27, 2026 • 5 min read

Harvested from traditional hives on the slopes of Mount Ngiro, our raw wild honey is more than just a sweet treat—it is a powerful natural medicine revered by Samburu elders for generations.

What Makes Wild Honey Different?

Unlike commercial honey found in supermarkets, raw wild honey is completely unprocessed. It is never heated, filtered, or pasteurized. This means it retains all of its natural enzymes, vitamins, and antioxidants. The bees forage on pristine, indigenous forest plants in an isolated ecosystem, giving the honey a uniquely rich flavor profile and unparalleled medicinal properties.

Key Health Benefits

  • Natural Immune Support: Packed with phytonutrients, raw honey acts as a powerful immune booster.
  • Rich in Antioxidants: Helps protect your body from cell damage due to free radicals.
  • Cough and Throat Relief: Traditionally used as an effective, natural remedy for sore throats and persistent coughs.
  • Digestive Health: Contains beneficial enzymes that support a healthy gut microbiome.

A Tradition of Sustainability

By choosing Ngiro Treasures wild honey, you aren't just improving your health; you are directly supporting local Samburu beekeepers. Our sustainable harvesting practices ensure that the sacred ecosystem of Mount Ngiro is preserved for future generations.
